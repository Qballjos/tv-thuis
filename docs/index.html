How it works

No root. No cloud. Just your TV.

  1. 1

    Allow it once

    Turn on developer options and accept the "Allow USB debugging?" prompt on the TV. That's the only permission it needs.

  2. 2

    It swaps the home screen

    Through the TV's own ADB (localhost, nothing leaves the TV) it becomes the home screen and screensaver, and turns off the stock launcher plus a short list of promo apps. Nothing is uninstalled.

  3. 3

    Undo anytime

    One button brings back the factory home screen, screensaver and every app. If something ever goes wrong, it switches back by itself.

Install

About ten minutes, start to finish.

Free, open source and easy to undo.

  1. On the TV: Settings → System → About → press Android TV OS build 7 times. Back in System, open Developer options and turn on USB debugging. Write down the TV's IP address (Settings → Network & Internet).
  2. On your computer: open an AI assistant (Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity) and say:
    Install TV Thuis (github.com/Qballjos/tv-thuis) on my TV at <TV-IP>. I live in <your city>.
    It downloads the code, installs the tools it needs, builds the app and puts it on your TV, and asks before anything important. Swap in your TV's IP and your city.
  3. Back on the TV: open TV Thuis → Home Screen → TV Thuis. That's when the TV asks Allow USB debugging? for TV Thuis: tick Always allow and accept.
  1. On the TV: turn on Developer options and USB debugging (as in the first tab), and note its IP address.
  2. On your computer: download FreeMyTV.apk from the latest release and install it with adb (on a Mac, brew install android-platform-tools):
    adb connect <TV-IP>:5555 && adb install -r FreeMyTV.apk
    On Android 14, if Play Protect holds it, choose More details → Install anyway. On TCL TVs, also let it run in the background (TCL blocks it by default):
    adb shell "appops set io.freemytv AUTO_START allow || appops set io.freemytv APP_AUTO_START allow"
  3. On the TV: open TV Thuis → Home Screen → TV Thuis, and when the TV asks Allow USB debugging?, tick Always allow and accept.
  4. The picks: Home Settings → TMDB key shows a QR code: scan it with your phone and paste your free TMDB key. Your city comes from the TV's time zone, and new versions install from Home Settings → Updates.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is this the official Free My TV?

No. TV Thuis is our household fork. The original project is simbasjungles/free-my-tv and the site freemytv.io. Use those if you want Free My TV itself.

What is AIO Media Manager?

The household media stack that feeds continue watching, recently added, trending and requests on this fork. It lives at Qballjos/aio-media-server-manager. TV Thuis signs in to it from Home Settings → AIO Media.

Is it safe?

It uses the same commands as Android's own developer tools, from inside the TV. Nothing is rooted or uninstalled: apps are only turned off and come back with one button. Picture, sound, inputs, the remote, Google services and the Play Store are on a never-touch list.

Which TVs does it work on?

Google TV and Android TV from Android 8 on: TCL, Sony, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi and more, streamers like Chromecast with Google TV and onn 4K, and LG and Samsung TVs as an app. The APK goes on the TV, not on your phone. On Huawei TVs (HarmonyOS) and Fire TV it stays an app you open, and the TV keeps its own home screen: on a Huawei, don't switch it.

How do I go back?

Open TV Thuis → Home Screen → Original. The factory home screen, screensaver and every app come back exactly as they were. If the new home screen ever crashes three times in five minutes, it switches back on its own.

Does it change picture quality?

No. Picture settings aren't touched, and videos play through the same hardware path. We measured the same resolution and the same video source in both modes.

What gets turned off?

The stock home screen and screensaver (replaced), Google TV's recovery home, Play Games and YouTube Music, and on TCL TVs the messages, store demo, hotel menu, manuals, mirroring and logging apps (and the like on Philco, Multilaser and NextStar TVs). Google Assistant only if you ask for it in the config.

Does it work on LG TVs?

Yes, from webOS 3 on. It installs through LG's own Developer Mode, or from the webOS Homebrew Channel, with nothing rooted or changed on the TV, and it opens with one press of a number key. Same home screen, same picks, weather and prices; for the picks, type your free TMDB key in its Home Settings. Press EXTEND in the Developer Mode app once a month to keep it.

Does it work on Samsung TVs?

Yes, on Samsung TVs from 2015 on. It installs through Samsung's own Developer Mode with TizenBrew Installer, the community's installer (2017 and newer), or as your own build, with nothing rooted or changed on the TV. Same home screen, weather and prices, with your city from the TV's time zone. For the trending picks, type your free TMDB key in its Home Settings.

Can I make it mine?

That's the idea. Open the folder in Claude Code and just ask: "add Crunchyroll to the shelf", "show prices in euros", "set it up for my Sony". It changes the code, rebuilds the app and puts it on your TV.

Does it speak my language?

English, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, German, French, Italian, Japanese and 18 more, following the TV's language, or the one you pick in its settings. Want another one? Ask Claude to add it.

Does it update itself?

On Google TV and Android TV, the ready APK looks for a new release once a week and installs it when you say OK, in Home Settings → Updates. On LG, the Homebrew Channel brings the new version; on Samsung, TizenBrew Installer installs the latest release over the one you have.

Does it collect any data?

No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. This fork also talks to our AIO media manager when configured. Otherwise it only talks to TMDB (picks), Open-Meteo (weather, for your city) and Binance (prices), and Watchmode (links to titles), your own Plex server, your own Immich server (your photos) and Trakt (what you're watching, on Google TV) only if you set them up; the Trakt sign-in stays on the TV. Your own Home Assistant only if you set its address and turn its buttons on. On Google TV and Android TV, the ready APK also asks GitHub once a week for a new version, and while Home Settings → TMDB key shows its QR code, the TV answers your phone on your home network (one key, 5 minutes at most).

Is it free?

Yes. MIT license, and the TMDB key is free too.
