# Free My TV: guide for AI assistants

For project development, repository maintenance, branches, GitHub Actions, and upstream merges, read
[PROJECT_SETUP.md](PROJECT_SETUP.md) first. Then read the relevant technical guidance in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md).

For installing or running Free My TV on someone's TV, follow [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). Most people ask in plain
words, like "Install Free My TV on my TV at <TV-IP>. I live in <your city>.", and don't know what a terminal is:
explain each step simply, and ask before installation actions that change their TV or computer.

That installation approval rule does not apply to routine project development requested by the owner.
